## Methodological Limitations

**IMPORTANT**: The labels in this dataset were generated by the C# analytical simulator.
This creates a circularity issue: the models are learning to reproduce the analytical
formula, not generalizing to real-world scenarios. High accuracy should be interpreted
as "the model learned the analytical formula well" rather than "the model is intelligent".
